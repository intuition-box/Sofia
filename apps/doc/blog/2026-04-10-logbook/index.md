## EthCC[9] & ETHGlobal Hackathon

EthCC[9] was a great opportunity to connect with builders, founders, and researchers
from across the ecosystem. The conversations we had reinforced a lot of what we're
building toward with Sofia.

On the hackathon side, we shipped [Wispear](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/wispear-cy889)
— a trust-scored discovery engine for AI agent components built on Dynamic and
Intuition Protocol.

## Semi-Off Week

After the event, we took intentional time to decompress and think. No shipping pressure —
just space to reflect on where Sofia is heading and what we want to prioritize next.
A few bugs were also fixed along the way.
