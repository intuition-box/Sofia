# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: a crypto-curious person arriving from Intuition, X or Discord who has never used Sofia. They land on a page cold, often from a shared link ("you earned a reward"), and must understand in about five seconds what Sofia gives them and how to get it, then make a first free Recommend.

Secondary: the returning hunter who comes back daily to check missions, credits, rank and fresh pages to spot before others. Every surface serves the newcomer first without slowing the regular down.

## Product Purpose

Sofia turns "I found this page before everyone else" into an on-chain, owned position on the Intuition protocol. One market per page (`I | recommends | page`): being early on a page that credible accounts later follow earns Flair, calls, free Recommends and, for the monthly Top 10, a share of a TRUST pool. Success: a newcomer understands the loop, claims what is theirs, and recommends a page within the first visit.

## Brand Personality

Playful, quick, competitive. The energy of a treasure hunt and a ranking people want to climb, with rewards that are visible and concrete. Confident and specific: say exactly what you get and why ("+3 free Recommends per call"), never vague hype.

## Anti-references

- Casino / memecoin: confetti, blinking counters, promises of gains.
- Dense DeFi dashboards: walls of numbers and tables unreadable for a newcomer.
- Generic SaaS: identical card grids, hero-metric template, purple gradients.
- Over-gamified quests: badges and progress bars everywhere (the retired Gold / XP system).

## Design Principles

1. One reason per screen: each page leads with the single thing the visitor can get or do there; everything else is secondary or lives elsewhere.
2. Show what is yours first: owned value (free Recommends left, positions, rewards) before rankings and other people.
3. Explain by example, not by paragraph: a concrete number or a real page beats a sentence of rules.
4. Every element earns its place: if information exists on another surface where it matters more, it does not repeat here.
5. Rewards are real and specific: amounts, counts and conditions are exact; no inflated or decorative figures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at 4.5:1, honest focus states, every animation (counters, marquee) has a reduced-motion alternative. Color is never the only carrier of meaning (gains and losses also carry a sign).
